First, two words you'll see everywhere

What is a "provider" and what is a "model"?

Before anything else makes sense, you need these two words. Think of it like ordering food:

🏢

A "provider" is the company

Just like Domino's or Pizza Hut are different pizza companies, Alibaba, Pixazo, Cloudflare, and Transloadit are different companies that make AI image tools. Each one runs its own kitchen, so quality, speed, and rules are different.

🍕

A "model" is the specific recipe

Inside one company's menu, there are different items — thin crust, deep dish, stuffed crust. A model is the specific AI "recipe" a provider offers. One provider can have several models, each better at different things.

🔑 Why this matters to you

This app doesn't force you to use one AI — it lets you choose the company (provider) and the specific version (model) for every image. That's more choice than ChatGPT or Gemini give you, but it also means you have to make a couple of small decisions they usually make for you.

Set expectations

How this is different from ChatGPT or Gemini

ChatGPT and Gemini each give you one built-in image tool. You just type what you want, in one chat box, and it quietly picks the size and style for you.

This app is more like a control room with several tools laid out in front of you. That gives you more power, but it means a few things you're used to being "automatic" now need a click from you — especially the size of the image, explained next.

The #1 thing that confuses new users

Picking image size & image shape

In ChatGPT or Gemini, you can just type something like "make it tall" or "a wide banner" and it usually figures out the size on its own. This app does not read size instructions from your text — you need to set it yourself, using the dropdown menus on the page.

Two settings to know

SettingIn plain wordsWhere to set it
Size
(sometimes called "resolution")		 How tall and how wide the picture is, measured in pixels — like 1024 height × 1024 width. Bigger numbers = a bigger, more detailed image. A "Height / Width" or "Custom size" option in the form.
Image shape
(sometimes called "aspect ratio")		 The overall shape of the picture — square, wide (like a TV screen), or tall (like a phone screen). An "Image shape" or "Aspect ratio" dropdown in the form.
⚠️ Common mistake

Typing "make this a tall, phone-shaped picture" in your prompt will not reliably work. Instead, leave your prompt describing what's in the picture, and use the Image Shape / Size dropdown on the page to actually set the shape and size.

Good to know: not every model lets you type an exact height and width. Some only offer a few ready-made shapes (square, wide, tall) to choose from. If you don't see a "custom size" option, just pick the closest shape from the list — that's normal, not a bug.

Changing an existing picture

Editing an image — which company to use

The Edit tab lets you upload or link a picture and describe a change (e.g. "change the background to a beach"). Two companies offer this: Alibaba and Transloadit.

🪫

Alibaba Runs out fast

Good quality, but each of its editing tools only lets you make about 10 edits before it stops working for a while. Fine to try once, not ideal if you're editing the same picture over and over.

🔋

Transloadit Recommended

Lets you make far more edits without running out. This is the better everyday choice for editing photos.

✅ Simple rule

When editing a picture, choose Transloadit as your company. Only use Alibaba's editing tools for a quick, one-time test.

Making new pictures from scratch

Which company should I pick to generate an image?

All four companies can create brand-new images from a text description, but they're not equally good at everything:

Inside Transloadit's menu

Which model should I pick? (Transloadit)

Transloadit doesn't build its own AI — instead it gives you a menu of well-known AI models from other companies. Two of them are ones you may already recognize:

Model name in the appWhat it really isPick it when…
google/nano-banana (and the "2" / "pro" versions) This is Google's own picture-making AI — the same technology behind Gemini's image tool. You want results that look and feel like what Gemini would give you.
openai/gpt-image-2 This is OpenAI's picture-making AI — the same one used inside ChatGPT. You want ChatGPT-style results, and especially when you need clean, readable text inside the picture (posters, signs, labels).
flux models Made by a separate company called Black Forest Labs — not Google or OpenAI. You want photo-realistic detail (the "Pro" version) or a fast, quick draft (the "Schnell" version).
recraft-v3 Made by Recraft — known for clean, flat, design-style pictures. You want logos, icons, or simple flat illustrations rather than realistic photos.
💡 Easy way to remember

Want it to look like Gemini made it? Pick a nano-banana model.
Want it to look like ChatGPT made it? Pick gpt-image-2.
Everything else on the list is its own separate AI, not a copy of a chatbot.

Putting it together

A simple first-time walkthrough

  1. Go to the "Generate" tab and pick Alibaba as the company.It's a safe, good-quality default. Describe what you want in plain words — subject, setting, mood.
  2. Set your image size and shape before pressing the button.Don't type it into your description — use the dropdowns.
  3. Want to change an existing picture?Go to "Edit," choose Transloadit as the company, pick a nano-banana model or gpt-image-2, then upload or link your picture.
  4. Need readable words inside the picture?Use Transloadit's gpt-image-2 model — not Cloudflare.
  5. Just want to play around for free?Try Pixazo while it's still free, or Cloudflare for pictures that don't need text.
  6. Like a prompt you wrote?Use the "Save prompt" button so you can reuse it later instead of retyping.
Quick answers

Common questions

Why did my picture come out the wrong shape?

You probably described the shape in your text instead of setting it in the dropdown. Set the "Image shape" or "Size" option on the page, not in your prompt.

Why did editing suddenly stop working?

If you were using Alibaba, you likely used up its ~10 free edits for that model. Switch to Transloadit and keep going.

Why does the text/writing in my picture look like gibberish?

You're probably using Cloudflare, which struggles with lettering. Switch to Alibaba, or Transloadit's gpt-image-2 for the cleanest text.

Is this the same as ChatGPT or Gemini's image tool?

Not exactly — this app is a control panel that connects to several different companies. Two of the models it offers (nano-banana and gpt-image-2) happen to be the very same AI used by Gemini and ChatGPT. The rest are separate AI tools with their own strengths.

Will Pixazo always be free?

No — its free access is temporary. Don't rely on it staying free long-term.

Do I need to log in or have my own API key?

No. The app is ready to use as-is — no login and no personal API key needed.
